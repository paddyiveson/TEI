How we work: coaching, not advice

The Everyday Investor provides financial coaching and education. We help you understand how your money works so that you can make your own decisions with confidence.

We are not a regulated financial advice service. The Everyday Investor is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). We do not provide regulated financial advice, discretionary investment management, or personal recommendations within the meaning of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

The Everyday Investor is an independent business. It is not part of, and does not act on behalf of, any regulated financial firm.

What we do

  • Explain how financial products, accounts and tax rules work, in plain English.
  • Help you understand where you are today and what your options are.
  • Show how different choices could play out, using forecasts and tools built on clearly stated assumptions.
  • Set out the trade-offs, including the risks and the bear case, for every option.
  • Help you build the knowledge to explain and justify your own decisions.

What we do not do

  • Tell you which investment, fund, pension or product to buy, sell or hold.
  • Make decisions for you, or manage your money on your behalf.
  • Hold your money or investments, or place trades for you.
  • Arrange insurance, mortgages or pension transfers.
  • Give regulated tax or legal advice.

Your decisions are yours

All sessions, communications and written materials, including Wealth Plans, Forecasts and tools, are educational and informational. You remain fully responsible for every financial decision you make, including any decision made after a coaching session.

When you need regulated advice

Some decisions need an FCA-authorised adviser. These include transferring a pension (especially one with guarantees or a final salary), arranging protection insurance, arranging a mortgage, and choosing specific investments for you. When a conversation reaches one of these points, we will say so clearly. You can check whether a firm is authorised on the FCA Register, and find free, impartial guidance at MoneyHelper.

What this means for your protection

Because we are not regulated by the FCA, you cannot take a complaint about our coaching to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme does not cover our service. We never hold your money, so your savings and investments keep whatever protection their own provider gives them. If you are unhappy with our service, contact us directly and we will put it right.
