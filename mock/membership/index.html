02 Coaching The human part first. Steps sit on the band; the sample plan is the one feature card. Its line draws once in view.

Coaching, one to one.

Every membership starts the same way, and your coach stays with you after.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    30 minutes on where you are and how membership works. No pitch at the end.

  2. 02

    Fact find

    The full picture: income, spending, accounts, pensions, debts and goals. About an hour, by form or together.

  3. 03

    Setup sessions

    As many as it takes, usually 3 to 4. Wealth OS is set up with your own figures.

  4. 04

    Your written plan

    A Wealth Plan and Forecast, with your strategy worked through, within three months.

After setup

  • ReviewsFull sessions at your tier's rhythm.
  • Written notesAfter every session.
  • Next stepsSet by your coach in Wealth OS.
  • MessagesBetween sessions, whenever something changes.
