What Is Ondas?

Ondas Inc. (NASDAQ: ONDS) is a West Palm Beach-based defence and autonomous systems company that completed one of the most dramatic transformations in small-cap defence history during 2025–2026. Through eight strategic acquisitions it has assembled a full-spectrum autonomous systems platform - connecting aerial drones, ground robots, stratospheric ISR, loitering munitions, counter-drone technology, and AI command software into a single integrated architecture.

It operates through two segments. Ondas Autonomous Systems (OAS) is the high-growth engine spanning every layer of autonomous defence. Ondas Networks provides the secure FullMAX software-defined wireless backbone - a sleeping dragon of recurring mission-critical revenue embedded in rail, utilities, and critical infrastructure.

The strategic logic mirrors early Palantir: don't build one great product - build the operating system that runs across every domain, irreplaceable once embedded. Ondas is co-developing its AI mission autonomy platform (SkyWeaver) directly with Palantir. What PLTR did for data intelligence, ONDS is doing for the physical battlefield.

What Drives the Re-Rating

The path from ~$4.9B today to $10B+ is not a moonshot - it is what happens when Q2 earnings confirm the revenue trajectory, Cyberhawk closes, and at least one major Drone Dominance or Golden Dome award is announced. Eight out of eight analysts agree the current price represents significant undervaluation on a 12-month horizon.

The larger re-rating - from defence roll-up ($10-15B) to platform compounder ($30-100B) - happens when Ondas demonstrates two things simultaneously: recurring software revenue from the C2 layer (Omnisys BRO, LADOS, SkyWeaver) and international programme wins that show the platform is not US-government-dependent. The ONBERG European JV, Israeli demining programmes, and Cyberhawk's 40-country commercial footprint are the first proof points of that broader thesis.

For context: Palantir's market cap journey went from ~$8B at IPO (2020) to $200B+ today - a 25x move over five years, driven entirely by the market re-rating a government-software platform as a commercial compounder. If Ondas executes the same transition from pure defence platform to dual-use autonomous systems leader, the long-term ceiling is genuinely very large.

Key Risks

324% share dilution Pre-profitability M&A integration Contract timing Rich valuation

Dilution is the most significant per-share risk. Shares outstanding rose 324% over the past year. Track revenue per share and backlog per share alongside headline figures.

Eight acquisitions in 18 months is demanding. Integration across Israel, Germany, US, and UK - spanning defence, engineering, and commercial inspection - introduces meaningful execution risk.

Government contract timing is unpredictable. Budget appropriations and continuing resolutions can move revenue between quarters without warning.

This is a high-conviction, high-volatility position. Position sizing should reflect that. The bull case rests on rapid integration and execution - if either slips, the share price reaction can be sharp.

Bottom Line

Ondas is assembling the autonomous operating system for modern defence - eight acquisitions, five domains, one AI command layer. The $400B TAM (Oppenheimer), the Palantir partnership, the Lockheed Martin integration, and the $4.3B global pipeline are the early proof points of a genuinely large long-term opportunity.

Near term: Eight analysts at Strong Buy, average target $20, HC Wainwright at $25. August Q2 earnings + Cyberhawk close + one major programme award = the re-rating to $10B+ market cap.

Long term: If the platform thesis executes - recurring software revenue from the C2 layer, international programme wins, Cyberhawk commercial scale - the Palantir comparison becomes less metaphor and more roadmap. $50–100B is the ceiling on a decade horizon.
