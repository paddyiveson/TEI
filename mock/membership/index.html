08 Is it for you? Two sides, with the quick check inside the feature box. Never gated, nothing stored. The testimonial slot stays hidden.

Is membership right for you?

Three quick questions, and an honest answer either way.

Do you want to understand your money well enough to make your own decisions?

Are your pension, ISAs, savings and debts still separate pieces, rather than one plan?

Would it help to have someone to check in with as life changes?

Mostly yes: membership is built for this, so start with a call. Mostly no: start with our free guides and tools first.

It is not the right fit if
  • You want someone to pick investments or manage your money for you.
  • You need regulated advice, for example a pension transfer or a mortgage.
  • You are looking for tips on what to buy next.

  • You make every decision.

    We explain how it works, never what to buy.

  • No products, no commission.

    You pay for coaching only.

  • A qualified coach.

    A planner holding the CII Diploma in Financial Planning. Read our story

How does the discovery call work?

We talk through where you are and how membership works, then decide together whether it is a fit. The call is £30, paid when you book, and comes off your first month if you join. You get it back in full if you cancel before the call. Rather talk first? Message us on WhatsApp.

Which tier should I start with?

It depends on how much support you want after setup. Core suits people who are confident with their plan, Momentum adds a review every quarter, and Inner Circle adds monthly check-ins. We talk it through on the call, and you can change tier any time.

Is membership financial advice?

No. TEI provides coaching and education. We explain how things work and show how choices could play out with your own figures. We never tell you which product to buy, sell or hold, and every decision stays yours. Coaching, not advice

What if I only want one session?

Membership will not suit everyone. Get in touch, tell us what you need, and we will talk it through and agree the support and the cost with you. Message us on WhatsApp
