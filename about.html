The founder

Most of the people I grew up with were never shown how money works. Not because they could not understand it. Because nobody ever took the time to explain it. That silence is why I went into financial services and earned the Diploma in Financial Planning, and it is why I started TEI: an independent business built to break it.

I have been interested in investing for as long as I can remember. Not in a spreadsheet-and-suit way. I just always wanted to understand how money actually worked, and why it seemed to work so much harder for some people than others.

That curiosity took me into financial services. And once I was actually inside the industry, something started to bother me. I would go home, talk to friends, family, people I had grown up with, and realise how little any of them actually knew. Not because they were not capable. Because nobody had ever bothered to sit down and explain it to them properly.

The same patterns kept showing up. Savings sitting in an account, quietly losing value, because it felt safer than the alternative. Workplace pensions nobody had looked at since the day they were auto-enrolled. People who assumed investing was for someone else, someone with more money, more time, more confidence than they had. None of it was stupidity. It was just silence. Nobody had ever offered to close the gap.

I still work inside financial services day to day, and that is exactly where I keep noticing it. The Everyday Investor is built around what that work has taught me, but it stands apart from it entirely. This is coaching and education, not regulated financial advice, and never a replacement for it.

Honestly, it started to frustrate me. I would sit there seeing the gap in someone's understanding, and know that if I did not say something, they would probably never hear it from anyone else either.

That is an uncomfortable thing to sit with once you have noticed it.

I did not build this because I had some grand plan. I built it because I got tired of watching people I cared about miss out on things that were not actually complicated, they just needed someone to explain them. That is still the whole point. Not a product. Not a pitch. Just someone willing to do that properly, and stay around while you put it into practice.

Coaching, not advice

The Everyday Investor provides financial coaching and education, not regulated financial advice. It is an independent business and does not act for any regulated firm.

What we do

  • Explain how financial products, accounts and tax rules work, in plain English.
  • Help you understand where you are today and what your options are.
  • Show how different choices could play out, using forecasts and tools built on clearly stated assumptions.
  • Set out the trade-offs, including the risks and the bear case, for every option.
  • Help you build the knowledge to explain and justify your own decisions.

What we do not do

  • Tell you which investment, fund, pension or product to buy, sell or hold.
  • Make decisions for you, or manage your money on your behalf.
  • Hold your money or investments, or place trades for you.
  • Arrange insurance, mortgages or pension transfers.
  • Give regulated tax or legal advice.

The Everyday Investor provides financial coaching and education. We help you understand how your money works so that you can make your own decisions with confidence.

When you need regulated advice

Some decisions need an FCA-authorised adviser. These include transferring a pension (especially one with guarantees or a final salary), arranging protection insurance, arranging a mortgage, and choosing specific investments for you. When a conversation reaches one of these points, we will say so clearly. You can check whether a firm is authorised on the FCA Register, and find free, impartial guidance at MoneyHelper.

What this means for your protection

Because we are not regulated by the FCA, you cannot take a complaint about our coaching to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme does not cover our service. We never hold your money, so your savings and investments keep whatever protection their own provider gives them. If you are unhappy with our service, contact us directly and we will put it right.

Your decisions are yours

All sessions, communications and written materials, including Wealth Plans, Forecasts and tools, are educational and informational. You remain fully responsible for every financial decision you make, including any decision made after a coaching session.
