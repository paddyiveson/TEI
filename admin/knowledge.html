Portfolio Blueprint

TEI's diversification and position-sizing philosophy, and what job each holding is meant to do.

Compounder

Exceptional business, held long term. Quality over excitement.

Growth Opportunity

Large market opportunity, meaningful uncertainty, early-to-mid stage.

Undervalued Opportunity

Price disconnected from value. Cyclical bets sit here as a descriptor (see the Cyclical flag on an Investment Record), not a separate role.

Turnaround / Special Situation

Change creates the opportunity. Higher risk, needs a defined failure point.

Defensive / Resilience

Stability and predictable demand. Lets the rest of the portfolio stay invested through uncertainty.

Income / Cash Generation

Dividend and cash-flow focus.

Conviction is not position size

Investment quality → Conviction → Portfolio decision → Position size are four separate judgement calls, never one flowing automatically into the next. A Core-conviction idea doesn't mean a large position — valuation, concentration, portfolio construction, and an individual client's circumstances all sit between a conviction band and an actual allocation.
