Questions

How does the discovery call work?

The first step is a 30-minute call. We talk through where you are and how membership works, then decide together whether it is a fit. The call is £30, paid when you book. It comes off your first month if you join, and you get it back in full if you cancel before the call. It is free if you were referred. Rather talk first? Message us on WhatsApp.

Which tier should I start with?

It depends on how much support you want after setup. Core suits people who are confident with their plan, Momentum adds a review every quarter, and Inner Circle adds monthly check-ins. We talk it through on the discovery call, and you can change tier any time with a month's notice.

Is membership financial advice?

No. TEI provides coaching and education. We explain how things work and show how choices could play out with your own figures. We never tell you which product to buy, sell or hold, and every decision stays yours. Coaching, not advice
