                The Everyday Investor

                Let's start with the basics

                Nothing here is regulated financial advice — it just helps your dashboard speak to you directly.

                Your partner

                Enter your own individual accounts and income in this onboarding — not combined household figures. Once your partner also has a TEI profile, your adviser can link the two into a household, and shared costs (rent, bills, anything you both pay for) can then be tagged as joint so they're counted once at the household level, not duplicated or missed on either dashboard.

                Money in, money out

                Rough numbers are fine for now — detailed budgeting comes later. This is what your dashboard and financial-freedom target are built from.

                Income

                Take-home pay (after tax and any salary sacrifice) — not gross salary.

                Fixed expenses

                Mortgage/rent, utilities, insurance — the costs that don't change month to month.

                Variable expenses

                Lifestyle spending, general spending, anything else that moves around.

                Emergency fund

                A cash buffer before anything else gets invested. Most people aim for 3–6 months of essential costs.
                Your monthly saving & investing rate is calculated automatically from the accounts you add next — no need to enter it separately.

                What do you own and owe?

                Approximate figures are fine — this becomes your net worth picture.

                Property

                Do you own property?

                Other assets

                Anything you own that isn't held in an investment account — vehicles, collectibles, business interests, and so on. Cash, ISAs, GIAs and pensions are captured as accounts on the next step.

                Liabilities

                Mortgage, loans, credit — what you owe.

                Your investment accounts

                Do you have any investment accounts — ISA, pension, GIA, LISA?

                What's inside those accounts?

                Entirely optional — skip this if you're not sure yet or don't have it to hand. You (or your adviser) can add it anytime.

                What are you working towards?

                A house deposit, financial independence, a tax-efficiency action — whatever it is. Add one or a few.
                Linked account(s)

                Any documents to keep handy?

                Statements, reports, anything worth having on file. Entirely optional — skip if you don't have anything to hand.

                      Accounts

                      ISA, GIA, pension, LISA, Cash ISA, current/deposit or other. Current value seeds this month's balance automatically — adjust it below for later months.
                      Pension splits personal/salary-sacrifice. Total contributions to date = pre-existing lump sum, powers Investment return right away. Cash balance = uninvested cash, added on top of holdings so totals reconcile. Interest rate (Cash ISA/Deposit only) accrues monthly — Fixed + Maturity date stops it at term. Joint account appears once linked to a household; stays edited here, read-only elsewhere.

                      Upload statements, screenshots or PDFs

                      There is no automated OCR or PDF extraction in this build — no backend to run it against. Files queue here for manual review: open the file, key the figures into Manual Entry, then mark it approved. This keeps the audit trail honest rather than pretending to extract data that was never actually read.
