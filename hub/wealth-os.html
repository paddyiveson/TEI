                                      Questions people ask

                                      Where do my figures come from?

                                      From what you and your coach enter: your accounts, balances, income and spending. US-listed shares are priced automatically; everything else is the value you enter from your statements.

                                      What should I update each month?

                                      Your balances (Update balances on Wealth) and how the month went (Update this month on Cashflow). It takes a few minutes and keeps every projection worth trusting.

                                      Is any of this financial advice?

                                      No. Wealth OS shows the mechanics of your money so you can make your own decisions. Projections are illustrative, not predictions or recommendations.

                                      The Everyday Investor provides financial coaching and education. We do not give regulated financial advice, and nothing in Wealth OS is a personal recommendation. If you need advice, we can point you to a regulated adviser.

                                      Your partner

                                      Enter your own individual accounts and income in this onboarding, not combined household figures. Once your partner also has a TEI profile, your coach can link the two into a household, and shared costs (rent, bills, anything you both pay for) can then be tagged as joint so they are counted once at the household level, not duplicated or missed on either dashboard.

                                      Money in, money out

                                      Rough numbers are fine for now, detailed budgeting comes later. This is what your dashboard and financial-freedom target are built from.

                                      Income

                                      Take-home pay (after tax and any salary sacrifice), not gross salary.

                                      Fixed expenses

                                      Mortgage/rent, utilities, insurance, the costs that do not change month to month.

                                      Variable expenses

                                      Lifestyle spending, general spending, anything else that moves around.

                                      Emergency fund

                                      A cash buffer before anything else gets invested. Most people aim for 3–6 months of essential costs. Set your target here, once you have added a cash account (next), link it in Cashflow settings so "currently have" always matches its real balance.
                                      Your monthly saving & investing rate is calculated automatically from the accounts you add next, no need to enter it separately.

                                      What do you own and owe?

                                      Approximate figures are fine, this becomes your net worth picture.

                                            ISA, GIA, pension, LISA, Cash ISA, current/deposit or other. Current value seeds this month's balance automatically, adjust it below for later months.
                                            More options
                                            Total contributions to date is calculated from monthly deposits plus any contributions before tracking started. Cash balance = uninvested cash, added on top of holdings so totals reconcile. Interest rate (Cash ISA/Deposit only) accrues monthly, Fixed + Maturity date stops it at term. Joint account appears once linked to a household; stays edited here, read-only elsewhere. Pension splits personal/salary-sacrifice.

                                            Recurring contribution,

                                            Applied automatically on the day each month: each fund's value and cost basis rise by its share. Statement values entered later replace the value. No back-dating, a change applies from the next run date.
