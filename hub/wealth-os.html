                The Everyday Investor

                Let's start with the basics

                Nothing here is regulated financial advice — it just helps your dashboard speak to you directly.

                Your partner

                Money in, money out

                Rough numbers are fine for now — detailed budgeting comes later. This is what your dashboard and financial-freedom target are built from.

                Income

                Take-home pay (after tax and any salary sacrifice) — not gross salary.

                Fixed expenses

                Mortgage/rent, utilities, insurance — the costs that don't change month to month.

                Variable expenses

                Lifestyle spending, general spending, anything else that moves around.

                Saving & investing

                Everything you're setting aside each month, including your own pension contributions (not your employer's).

                Emergency fund

                A cash buffer before anything else gets invested. Most people aim for 3–6 months of essential costs.

                What do you own and owe?

                Approximate figures are fine — this becomes your net worth picture.

                Property

                Do you own property?

                Other assets

                Anything you own that isn't held in an investment account — vehicles, collectibles, business interests, and so on. Cash, ISAs, GIAs and pensions are captured as accounts on the next step.

                Liabilities

                Mortgage, loans, credit — what you owe.

                Your investment accounts

                Do you have any investment accounts — ISA, pension, GIA, LISA?

                What's inside those accounts?

                Entirely optional — skip this if you're not sure yet or don't have it to hand. You (or your adviser) can add it anytime.

                What are you working towards?

                A house deposit, financial independence, a tax-efficiency action — whatever it is. Add one or a few.
                Linked account(s)

                Any documents to keep handy?

                Statements, reports, anything worth having on file. Entirely optional — skip if you don't have anything to hand.

                      Upload statements, screenshots or PDFs

                      There is no automated OCR or PDF extraction in this build — no backend to run it against.
