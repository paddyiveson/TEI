The reality

Your money is losing ground.
Every single day.

Most people know they should be investing, but there is always a reason to delay. Here is what it is costing you…

Three reasons this keeps happening:

01

Cash savings lose ground every year

At 3% inflation, £10,000 in a savings account is worth under £7,400 in ten years. Safe on paper. Shrinking in reality.

02

Most investors either do too little or too much

Putting everything into one fund and hoping is not a strategy. Neither is chasing hot tips. Both are common. Neither is deliberate.

03

The financial industry is not built for everyday investors

Regulated advice is priced for people who already have significant wealth. Everyone else is left choosing between get-rich-quick schemes that put their money at risk, and professional material that assumes a level of knowledge they do not have yet.
